BEFORE YOUR VISIT

Helpful details.

Scheduling, pricing and payments.

Do you offer 24/7 emergency plumbing service?

Yes. For emergency plumbing or sewer service, call our emergency line at (773) 471-1444, any time of day or night. Describe the issue and confirm dispatch availability.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve the entire Chicagoland area, including Darien, Hinsdale, Naperville, Bolingbrook, Joliet, Orland Park, Burr Ridge, La Grange, Elmhurst, Downers Grove and Woodridge. Call with your address to arrange service.

How does your pricing work?

We offer flat-rate pricing so you know your pricing upfront, which is very fair. The $125 dispatch fee applies and is always credited towards the work performed. Again, a very fair policy. The proposed work and cost are explained before you approve the repair. Please call (630) 663-1444 now for your estimate.

Is there an extra charge on Saturdays?

There is no overtime rate for Saturdays, we are not Pre-Madonnas. Extended service hours are available; Please call to discuss scheduling.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, checks, Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Discover. Payment is due on completion.

Are you licensed and insured?

Ed’s Plumbing & Sewer is fully licensed, bonded and insured.
