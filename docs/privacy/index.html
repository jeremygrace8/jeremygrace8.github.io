Your privacy.

A straightforward explanation of this website’s contact options.

Let’s talk about service

Contact inquiries

The service inquiry form prepares an email in your own email application. It does not submit or store information on this website. You choose whether to send the email, and your email provider handles that transmission.

Information you email or provide by phone is received by Ed’s to respond to your inquiry and arrange service. Avoid including payment card information in an email request.

Cookies and analytics

This static website includes no advertising trackers, analytics scripts or optional cookies in its own code. The hosting provider may process connection information needed to serve the website or provide access to a private preview.

Embedded video

The About page includes a Vimeo video player. Loading or playing the video connects to Vimeo, which may process connection information and use cookies for its player. See Vimeo’s privacy policy for details.
