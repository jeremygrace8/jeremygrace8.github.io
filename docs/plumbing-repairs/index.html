Leaks & plumbing repairs

From a persistent drip to low water pressure, we look beyond the symptoms to provide the best repair your plumbing needs. Get clear advice from our expertly trained team before the repair begins.

Let’s talk about service

Small drips deserve expert attention.

A thoughtful fix for everyday problems.

Ed’s locates water and sewer leaks, investigates the cause and explains the proposed repair, cost and applicable guarantee before proceeding with your approval. The team also helps with low-pressure problems around the home.

  • Water and sewer leak detection and repair
  • Low water pressure diagnosis
  • Faucet, sink and toilet replacement
  • Fixture installation and plumbing connections

Know what happens next

Describe where you see the problem, when it started and whether more than one fixture is affected. Ed’s will discuss the appropriate next step and arrange a visit. For an urgent leak, call the emergency line directly.

Clear pricing.
The next step.

Please call to discuss your project and arrange a visit. We provide you with a clear, understandable written proposal. We offer flat-rate, up-front pricing, always with your dispatch fee credited toward your final cost. We are known for our clear, easy to understand, written proposals.
