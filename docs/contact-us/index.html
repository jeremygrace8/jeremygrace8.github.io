BEFORE YOUR VISIT

Helpful details.

Scheduling, pricing and payments.

Do you offer 24/7 emergency plumbing service?

Yes. For emergency plumbing or sewer service, call Ed directly at (773) 471-1444, any time of day or night. Describe the issue and confirm dispatch availability.

Which areas do you serve?

Ed’s serves the metropolitan Chicago area, including Darien, Hinsdale, Naperville, Bolingbrook, Joliet, Orland Park, Burr Ridge, La Grange, Elmhurst, Downers Grove and Woodridge. Call with your address to arrange service.

How does your pricing work?

Ed’s offers flat-rate pricing. A dispatch fee applies and is credited toward the work performed. The proposed work and cost are explained before you approve the repair. Call for the current fee and an estimate.

Is there an extra charge on Saturdays?

There is no overtime rate for Saturdays. Extended service hours are available; call to discuss scheduling and the cost for your visit.

What payment methods do you accept?

Ed’s accepts cash, checks, Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Discover. Payment is due when service is performed.

Are you licensed and insured?

Ed’s Plumbing & Sewer is licensed, bonded and insured. Ask the team for current credentials or details relevant to your project when arranging service.

Plumbing emergency?

Available 24/7. Call Ed directly for urgent help.
